<h1 align="center">Angelgram</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Black, white and a touch of red. The power of Telegram, in your own style.</b></p>

---

**Angelgram** is a dark-themed Android messaging app that runs on the Telegram platform. Sign in with your existing Telegram account and your chats, groups, channels and files come along exactly as they are.

## Why Angelgram?

- **Dark and minimal.** The default theme is black and white with red accents. Easy on the eyes, comfortable at night.
- **A look of its own.** Wings inside a speech bubble: its own logo, name and launch animation.
- **Fast start.** A short launch animation of about a second, then straight to the login screen. No welcome tour.
- **Familiar interface.** Everything you know from Telegram is right where you left it. Nothing to relearn.

## Everything Telegram offers

- Unlimited chats, groups and channels
- Large files, photos and videos
- Voice and video calls
- Secret chats
- Stickers, emoji and reactions
- Stories
- Multiple accounts
- Messages synced across all your devices

## Technical notes

Angelgram is built on top of the official open-source [Telegram Android](https://github.com/DrKLO/Telegram) client and uses the Telegram API with its own API credentials. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram.

Telegram for Android is licensed under GPLv2, and this project is covered by the same license.
